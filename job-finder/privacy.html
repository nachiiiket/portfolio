Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

Job Finder is run by Nachiket Yawalkar ("we"). In short: your job search data stays in your browser, and we don't run a server that collects it.

What the extension reads

  • LinkedIn pages you open: the text of posts loaded on the page, including email addresses, author names, profile links and post links, so it can list posts that include an email.
  • Gmail: it opens Gmail's compose window and fills in the recipient, subject, body and resume attachment. It does not read your inbox.

What is stored, and where

These are kept in your browser's extension storage on your computer:

  • Posts you save: email address, author name and profile, post link, post text and dates.
  • Records of who you contacted and when.
  • Your email and message templates, and the resume you upload.
  • Your usage counts and license status.
  • Any AI provider API key you choose to add.

Your settings, such as roles, locations and templates, use Chrome's sync storage. Chrome may sync them across browsers where you're signed in, under Google's privacy terms.

Saved posts include contact details of other people. Please keep and use them only for your own job applications.

What leaves your browser

  • Emails you choose to send go through your own Gmail account.
  • License checks: your license key is sent to our license provider to confirm your plan.
  • Payments: our payment provider handles checkout and receives your payment details. We don't see or store full card numbers.
  • Optional AI query planner: if you add your own API key, your roles, locations and companies go to the AI provider you choose, such as OpenAI, OpenRouter or Groq, under that provider's terms.
  • Exports: CSV and Excel files are saved to your computer.
  • Support: if you email us, we keep your message and address to help you.

We don't sell your data, show ads or use tracking analytics in the extension.

Keeping or deleting your data

You control your data. Clear data in the dashboard deletes your saved posts, and removing the extension deletes its local storage. We keep payment and license records as long as tax and accounting rules require.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data we hold. Email us to use them. If you find your own contact details in someone's saved list, that list is on their computer and we can't access it, but you can ask them directly to remove it.

Children

Job Finder is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

We will change the date above when we update this policy.

    How it works

    The same steps the built-in tutorial walks you through the first time you open the panel.

    1. Set up your search

      Add your job roles, locations and email template in the dashboard, and upload your resume if you want it attached.

    2. Open a suggested search

      The panel on LinkedIn suggests searches from your roles. Press Search to open one, or Next query to move on. A progress bar shows how far through them you are.

    3. Scroll, and emails appear

      Scroll LinkedIn as you normally would. When a post on your screen includes an email, its author and address appear in the panel.

    4. Hide what you don't want

      Hide free domains like gmail, so posts from people who are open to work don't fill your list. Hide posts containing words you pick, like unpaid. Pro feature.

    5. Read the post

      Click a name to jump to that post. Read it and decide. If you already contacted that person, the panel shows the date.

    6. Send or save

      Use the buttons on the post: Send email or Draft opens Gmail with your template, and Save & track adds it to your dashboard. Nothing goes out unless you choose it.

    7. Track every application

      The dashboard keeps the author, their profile, the post link and the date you sent your email. Export it to CSV or Excel at any time.

    You stay in control

    Runs when you do

    It doesn't run searches, scroll or browse LinkedIn in the background. It works with the page you have open.

    You choose every recipient

    You pick who to contact, one at a time or by ticking several posts. Emails go out from your own Gmail account.

    Your list stays on your computer

    Saved posts, templates and your resume are kept in your browser. We don't run a server that collects them. See the privacy policy.

    Never email twice

    It remembers who you already contacted and stops you from emailing the same address again.

    Use it for personal job applications, not for bulk or unsolicited marketing. You're responsible for following LinkedIn's and Google's terms and anti-spam laws. See the terms.

    Get the extension

    Job Finder runs in Google Chrome on desktop.

    1. Install from the Chrome Web Store

      Job Finder is available exclusively through the Chrome Web Store. The store link will appear here as soon as the listing is published.

    2. Add your details

      Open the dashboard, add your roles and email template, and upload your resume.

    3. Open LinkedIn

      The panel appears on the right of the page. Press Tutorial for a guided tour.
